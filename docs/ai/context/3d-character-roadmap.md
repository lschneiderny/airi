## Where the 3D work lives

The 3D work is on `main`. It landed from `luke/feat/fullscreen-stage` on 2026-11-06 in six slices (PRs #13 to #17 and this page's update), under the trunk-and-flag structure in [Lazy 3D renderers](../adr/2026-11-04-lazy-3d-renderers.md):

- The MMD and VRM scenes load only when such a model is selected. `pnpm check:bundle` fails if three, three-mmd, three-vrm, or Tres reach the Live2D startup chunks, and the `airi/lazy-3d-renderers` lint rule rejects static imports of the 3D renderers outside their lazy entries.
- The Experimental 3D stage setting is off by default and gates only the desktop stage host: the transparent full-screen window, click-through, dragging, locomotion, home persistence, and floating-chat anchoring. MMD clips, curation, and the body work in the classic window whenever an MMD model is selected.
- `AIRI_STAGE_HOST=desktop` overrides the setting in development builds only.

## Decisions that shape the order

These decisions were taken on 2026-11-02 for M3 and recorded in `.omo/drafts/mmd-actions-library.md`:

- D1: M3 first, with the M4 window-rect spike in parallel in the same plan. M4 planning waits for both.
- D2: The space model is the ground line now, with a terrain layer added later. Nothing in M2 changes for it.
- D3: The model gets two channels: an ACT field for short masked gestures inline with speech, and the `perform_action` tool for full-body actions and dances, gated by the director. Only exposed clips reach the prompt.
- D4: Dances play at native rate. Beat matching is a follow-up.
- D5 to D7: Perching is decided by the director only, pointing is limited to the cursor, the chat and the foreground window, and props are split out until after M4.

## Idle agency (2026-11-12)

CM6a's D16 ruling of 2026-11-21 adds one corrective stroll after a cursor chase brakes beside a resting pointer. The body director issues it once she is idle, inside the attend window, beyond `STEP_ONTO_CURSOR_MIN_LEGS` (0.15 legs). The owner's follow-up ruling of 2026-11-21 makes that goal request `step: 'sidestep'`: within `SIDESTEP_MAX_LEGS` (0.6 legs, a tuning value) at departure, the gait keeps her yaw and takes leading/trailing step pairs onto the pointer. Farther goals still walk. The [character mind roadmap](character-mind-roadmap.md) tracks the press acceptance.

Outside the milestone order, the [idle agency ADR](../adr/2026-11-12-idle-agency.md) added self-directed behaviour to every Natural renderer: idle activities and sleep in `motion-driver-natural`, and for MMD unasked clips ("Idle may use"), visits toward the cursor, and lingering after a walk. On Live2D she can use the rig's own expressions unasked: pastimes such as a game, and what she sleeps with. The M4 window-rect sensor would replace the cursor as the target of a visit. The work is on `luke/feat/idle-agency` and is not visually reviewed.

Since PR 32, Natural's idle draw is the only idle scheduler. It also starts the Live2D pastimes, the MMD unasked clips, and the roam-mode wander, which had their own timers before. Each kept its old rate. The idle agency ADR records what replaced each timer.

## Whole-body performance (2026-11-11 to 2026-11-14)

Outside the milestone order, PR 32 (`luke/feat/natural-mmd-motion`) replaced the MMD body's separate rest pose and weight shift with the [whole-body performer](../adr/2026-11-11-whole-body-performer.md) in `motion-driver-humanoid/performance`. One layer stack poses the body for speech gestures, emotions, cues, reactions, idle acts, and the clip-free rest and doze. The walk drives the arms from the trunk's twist, each arm with its own drifting lag, and varies each stride by a sourced amount. Since the [natural walk refit](../adr/2026-11-23-natural-walk-refit.md) a walk turns while it steps off and while it brakes, and its arm swing reaches the arm followers through their inverse.

The idle acts include a two-hand clasp, a heel raise on a toe-down foot pitch, an arm-swing finger flick, and a hair tuck sized from ANSUR II. A capability gate keeps Natural from drawing an act that a rig cannot perform. On the 32 library rigs, measured over six seeds on 2026-11-25 against the body model's volumes and the author's head and neck bodies, every pair of arms reaches the clasp, one hand over the other, except Bronya's and that of Chisa in her swimsuit, and 27 rigs reach the hair tuck: all but both Lucilla forms, Sigrika and two Remielle variants. The [body model ADR](../adr/2026-11-25-body-model.md) has the comparison with the author's bodies alone.

Headless QA runs the real MMD adapter in Node: `pnpm qa:motion3d` makes seeded runs and run diffs, and `pnpm lab:motion3d` holds the [body motion quality gate](../adr/2026-11-11-body-motion-quality-gate.md), topic reports, clips, a run matrix, and sealed fault injection. Status: feature-complete; motion feel is not tuned yet. The decisions taken during the work are recorded in the amendments of the idle agency and whole-body performer ADRs.

### Not met yet

Scores prove direction, size relative to the recipe, release, and detectability above the rig's idle noise. They do not prove naturalness, which stays a visual review. Visual verdicts so far come from independent reviewers looking at the posed grey mesh (no face, hair or cloth physics), not from Luke. Some measures are printed but left out of verdicts and are checked by visual review instead: pause sway, the lean cue, release and onset of emotions, head-only emotions, and lasting state after an idle act (the idle report's `pair` line).

| Walk | Tuning. The [natural walk refit](../adr/2026-11-23-natural-walk-refit.md) of 2026-11-23 refitted the stride, the stance, the swing, the arm phase and the turns to motion capture of walking adults, and `walk-form` version 5 now judges arm opposition, the knee at heel strike and turns made while stepping. The desktop stage has replayed the rendered walk. Luke has not reviewed the result. The walk was run on 13 rigs; the ADR holds what held on all of them and the two foot shapes that still bob. On the 13 rigs, median knee flexion at heel strike is 16.7 to 18.8 degrees against a 20-degree target, and all 10 turns of the walk scenario are made while stepping (6 of 10 before the arrival turn started half a step ahead of the braking). Still open: the gate's shoulder and upper-arm mirror lines (0.98 to 0.997), its two dimension lines and its chest-neck line fail, since one signal still drives both arms and the trunk; `roll-every-step` (version 6, which finds steps along the path) still fails 1 or 2 steps a minute on 10 of 13 rigs. The failing steps are the arrival turn's steps, set down flat as designed once she has all but stopped (0.02 to 0.04 L/s, 30 to 50 degrees into the turn to face the viewer); along the path they stand 0.11 to 0.26 L from the other foot, so the report judges them as walking steps. Which stop holds one shifts with where the stop falls in the stride (a speed change of a few percent moved the count from 1 rig to 9); walking arms are held 14 to 22 degrees out on ten rigs against 12 to 15 in the reference, closer than the 19 to 37 of the 13-rig survey since the body proxies fit the rest hang ([walk upper body ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-walk-upper-body.md), whose amendment of 2026-11-25 nods the chest 2.5 degrees after the spine and the head 2.5 after the chest, and trails the elbow by 0.17 s), and the rest is each rig's own rest hang; on some rigs a braking step lifts the hips about 0.025 L in four frames as the trailing foot leaves the ground; the capture holds no standing start or stop, so those rest on judgment. The [walk reversal ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-walk-reversal.md) of 2026-11-24 makes a reversal one turn through the stop: she no longer stands facing the viewer between the two directions, and her head leaves first. Its amendment of 2026-11-25 turns over 2.8 steps at a steady rate, lands every walking foot within 55 degrees of the facings its stance sweeps and sets the outgoing step 0.2 L ahead of the brake foot, so no shoe swivels under her and the ankles no longer stack. The [walk start ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-walk-start-look.md) of the same day turns a start from standing over 2.0 steps with its gaze half a step ahead; its amendment of 2026-11-25 makes that turn about each stance hip in turn and turns a walking foot in the first 0.7 of its swing, so at the first foot's mid-swing her hips already stand 37 degrees round and the foot lands with them 48 degrees round. The [walk mood legs ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-walk-mood-legs.md) adds a push off the trailing foot, a loaded knee and a folded chest to the style, so a happy step springs, an angry one loads, a sad one folds and narrows. Its amendment of 2026-11-25 puts anger into a lifted, loaded plant and square shoulders instead of bent arms, lets fatigue load each step and rise out of it slowly with the head carried, and takes a wide swing from the shoulder, so the happy hand no longer rises to shoulder height; the tired walk still differs from neutral least. The [walk style ADR](../adr/2026-11-23-walk-style.md) of 2026-11-23 shapes the walk by her mood, energy and a character baseline through a `GaitStyle` whose neutral value is this walk; its gains await a visual review. The rig limits its step (a foot whose ball sits under the ankle walks with shorter steps, and slower when its top cadence cannot keep the step short) and eases the arm's back swing into the room behind the hip. A card stores her walking baseline in its profile (`gait`), set in the card editor from six presets or seven sliders, with a walk preview on the desktop stage. The [walk urgency ADR](../adr/2026-11-23-walk-urgency.md) replaces the two paces with an urgency per goal reason, so a wander ambles, a chat approach eases and a chase hurries with a slight forward lean. `lab:motion3d report walk-style` measures a walk's style beside the human range (`.omo/plans/walk-style.md`). Tension, fatigue and arousal lean her trunk forward: on three rigs angry and anxious walks lean 7.5 to 8.5 degrees and tired walks 7 degrees more than the neutral walk, against 11 to 14 in acted walks, because the lean bends the spine and not the pelvis. Knee swing peaks at 55 to 68 degrees against 61 to 79 in acted walks (normal 71): in mid-swing her hip rides 0.03 L higher than a walking adult's, since her stance leg is straighter, so the same foot lift bends the knee less. |
| Speech gestures | Tuning. Luke has not reviewed the result. `lab:motion3d report gesture-timing` judges only the `speak` scenario, whose utterances carry no pauses; the `speak-phrases` scenario carries pauses and is reviewed by eye. The 2026-11-25 amendment raises the hands only for an onset they stroke and strokes it once they arrive. A hand with nothing more to stress lets go while speech goes on, including one hand of two. ## Natural pep (2026-11-24)

Outside the milestone order, the [Natural pep ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-natural-pep.md) gives each card a `pep` value from 0 to 1. Pep adds an input-driven upper-body sway and livelier eyes to Natural on Live2D and MMD; on MMD the trunk takes a third of the sway's roll and the legs stay planted. Pep 0 is the Natural of before, and `qa:motion3d diff` prints `IDENTICAL` on all 12 scenarios. `pnpm lab:natural` measures the motion against reference bands. Open: the visual review listed in the plan (`.omo/plans/natural-pep.md`, section 6), a livelier groove, and whether any card starts above 0.

## Body model (2026-11-25)

Outside the milestone order, the [body model ADR](../adr/2026-11-25-body-model.md) replaced the author's rigid bodies as the description of her body from the hips to the shoulders and on the thighs. `motion-driver-humanoid` builds a renderer-neutral body model from the mesh and its skin weights: hard volumes, garments, limb radii and each arm's rest clearance. The MMD arm guard reads its volumes, and each arm's rest clearance is a trait of the rig that the performer rests at. On the 32 library rigs, 11 of the 14 that rested an arm inside a coat or skirt on `main` no longer do. Aria's two forms and Nicole stay pierced, and gesture paths still cross garments. The head and the neck keep the author's bodies, so the face-touch gestures reach what they reach on `main`. The resting arm carriage is not visually reviewed.

Later readers of the model wait for their milestones: the VRM adapter (M5) fills the same `BodyMeshSource`, and props and terrain contact (M4) read the hard volumes as her surface. A head fitted to the mesh, with a skull, a jaw and a face, is a reader of its own: one sphere on the head lost the hair tuck on 8 rigs. The [body model plan](../../../.omo/plans/body-model.md) lists them.

## Open across milestones

- Display hand-off: walking from one display to another. Built on 2026-11-10 ([display hand-off ADR](../adr/2026-11-10-display-hand-off.md)). A crossing passes `stage-handoff` in a two-output headless KWin session; the crossing on physical displays is not yet checked.

- A Live2D stage mode, which no milestone has scheduled.
