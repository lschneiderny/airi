# Autonomous behavior

This file describes the code on `main`. A change that makes a statement here false updates this file in the same commit. These rules win over the ADRs in `docs/ai/adr/`; the ADRs record the history behind them. When an ADR and this file disagree, this file describes the code.

This file lists every piece of code that makes the character do something nobody asked for, the order that applies today when two of them want the same moment or body part, and the places where two of them act on the same trigger or channel without one arbiter. Code is cited by repository path and symbol, never by line number. Terms follow the [domain glossary](glossary.md).

Every Natural [decider](glossary.md#decider) runs only while the [motion driver](glossary.md#motion-driver) is Natural.

## Precedence

The order applied today, from the outermost arbiter inward. Each step names the code that applies it.

1. A user turn always runs. It bypasses the gate, and the gate holds while the user speaks or types, while a turn generates, and while sends wait in the queue (`hardReason` in `packages/stage-shared/src/situation/gate.ts`).
2. One unprompted turn at a time. A reach-out, a widget reaction, and a spoken touch reaction share the chat store's single pending unprompted turn (`useChatStore` `submitUnprompted` and `submitReaction` in `packages/stage-ui/src/stores/chat.ts`). Inside the mind, they also share one open `line` (`MindVoiceState` in `packages/character-mind/src/types.ts`), which stays open until its `voice-outcome`. A waiting widget request goes before a reach-out: the gate skips evaluation while a line is open, a widget request waits, or another unprompted turn is pending, and the topics of waiting requests are held from the gate. A line that finds another unprompted turn pending is refused: `speak` of `useSituationStore` (`packages/stage-ui/src/stores/situation/index.ts`) resolves `refused` without sending, and a widget line's caller gets `failed`. For a touch line, the mind then takes back the retry wait and lets a protest's burst roll again (`settleReaction`); for a reach-out, it restores the gate memory from before the fire. The draws stay spent.
3. Within the urge gate: hard suppressors, then an urgent notify, then a due double text, (an urgent notify passes only the game hold), then the urge path with holds in the order `floor`, `quiet-unanswered`, `safety-cap`, `awaiting-reply`, `urge`, `no-topic` (`evaluateUrgeGate`). The gate runs inside the mind (`answerVoiceTick`). A spoken touch reaction or widget line starts the gate's floor inside the mind (`recordExternalSpeech` in `answerVoiceOutcome`), and a widget line never counts as an unanswered opening.
4. Natural performs only under the Natural driver. The conductor sends no idle drive to any other driver (`StagePerformanceConductor` `idleDrive`), and the MMD scene sends a rate of 0 while a clip action plays (`resolveIdleDrive` in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/components/scenes/MMD.vue`).
5. Inside one Natural grid step (`NaturalMotion` in `packages/motion-driver-natural/src/natural-motion.ts`):
   - The long-pet camera glance holds the gaze first. Then the gaze goes to an active pet override, then `aside` attention, then a chat glance or the chat fixation while the user types, then cursor attention (its weight scaled down by the pet level), then free fixation (`NaturalGazeLayer` `drawTarget` in `packages/motion-driver-natural/src/gaze.ts`).
   - Cursor attention is dismissed while its setting is off, attention is `aside`, or attention is `screen`.
   - Pending actions start in the order the renderer's adapter queued them (`startPending`), and the Live2D and MMD adapters queue them in different orders. An emotion gesture does not start while a cue gesture that started earlier holds the head.
   - Idle activities come last. They are blocked by the layers above and interrupted by any new poke, emotion, cue gesture, desktop reaction, noticed cursor, or active pet.
6. Within the idle draw: the cues `about-to-speak` and `let-go` go first, then a requested play, then sleep while the user is away, then a follow-up stretch or yawn after sleep, and only then a new draw; acts and errands share one hazard by rate, with one errand at a time; an `idle-clip` waits for a running act, while a pastime and a wander run beside acts (`NaturalIdleActivities`).
7. On Live2D expressions, slot leases from the model's requests outrank idle claims (pastimes, sleep entries, moments), which outrank the user's manual switches; a manual change drops the claims (`useIdleProps` in `packages/stage-ui/src/composables/performance/use-idle-props.ts`).
8. On the MMD body (`decideBody` in `packages/motion-driver-humanoid/src/director/index.ts`):
   - A request from any source other than `idle` replaces an unasked clip. An unasked clip is refused while anything performs, while she walks, and while she talks, listens, or thinks. Any clip is refused while she is away or in the sleep stance, or while the user drags her.
   - A performing clip other than a gesture forces a stand over sit or sleep; otherwise Natural's `asleep` maps to the sleep stance or a doze, and `settled` maps to sit or a rest, only at home and at rest.
   - Goal requests: debug over tool over chat unfold. Then a held goal, then the cursor chase, then return home, then entering a zone from a wing, then a wander or visit. While the user is active (`watching`), a wander only visits.
9. On the MMD gaze, walking outranks everything: travel direction, then the cursor while Natural fixates it, then the chat, then free fixation around the camera (`MMDBody` in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/composables/mmd/body/index.ts`).
10. One touch decider for each Live2D driver. Under Natural, the response the mind decides (rule 14) is the only response to a touch: the scene starts no tap or pat gesture, no touch reaction, no touch motion, and no avatar touch notify (`touchGesturesActive` in `packages/stage-ui-live2d/src/components/scenes/Live2D.vue`, `onAvatarTouch` in `packages/stage-ui/src/components/scenes/Stage.vue`). Under Universal and MAGIC, upstream's touch reaction, touch motion, and avatar touch notify respond, and the Natural touch row above does not run (`StagePerformanceConductor` `touch` returns unless the driver is Natural).
11. Every chat action runs in the window that holds the speech-player role, and in the leader when no window holds it (`inSpeechPlayer` in the `synced` options of `useChatStore`). A reply therefore generates where it is spoken.
12. A turn that does not speak does not count as anyone speaking. `speaks` is false for a background task turn and for a turn of a [scene session](glossary.md#scene-session) (`createChatOrchestratorRuntime` in `packages/core-agent/src/runtime/chat-orchestrator-runtime.ts`). The conversation and exchange sensors (`packages/stage-ui/src/stores/situation/sensors/conversation.ts`, `packages/stage-ui/src/stores/situation/sensors/exchange.ts`), the character state (`useCharacterStateStore`), and the stage's speech, expression, and thinking motion (`holdsVoice` in `Stage.vue`) skip such a turn. A notice is not owner speech: the same sensors skip its user message, and the stage does not count it as a turn of the user.
13. Recipes start only from the owner: a keyword in the owner's message, the use-recipe tool, or the judge tool of a decision recipe, each in the owner's own conversation (`keywordRecipesFor`, `withRunTools`, and `prepareSend` in `useChatStore`). No timer, sensor, or notify starts a recipe.
14. Under Natural, one touch gets one response, decided by `step` (`answerTouch` in `packages/character-mind/src/touch.ts`). The scene shows nothing for a touch until the mind answers. `StagePerformanceConductor` `touch` sends one `touch` stimulus and dispatches the intents in the mind's order: body (`reactToTouch`), mood (`addMood`), moment (`markMoment`), then line (`VoicePort` `speak`). `answerTouch` emits a `touch-reaction` for every touch, and the pet hand reaches Natural only between the mind's pet `start` and `end` (`naturalPetGranted` in `Model.vue`, `petGranted` in `MMD.vue`). The rule keeps Luke's touch rulings: a pet or poke gets a mood change or an expression every time, and a spoken line only occasionally. Pets melt into the hand: no look-up and no camera snap while a pet runs. A pet longer than 3 s ends with one camera glance of about 1.2 s (`longPetDurationSeconds` and `petGlanceDurationSeconds` in `NaturalMotion`). Sources: the [nonverbal channels ADR](../adr/2026-10-31-nonverbal-channels.md) context, decision D1 of the [cursor attention ADR](../adr/2026-10-24-live2d-cursor-attention.md) and decision A8 of the `.omo/drafts/live2d-cursor-touch-integration.md` draft.
15. One [return](glossary.md#return) gets one response, decided by `step` under every driver (`answerPresence` in `packages/character-mind/src/presence.ts`). `Stage.vue` hands the events of each `resolvePerformanceState` call to `StagePerformanceConductor` `desktop`, which sends them to the mind in one `step` call. The mind answers a return with a posture `wake` (a gentle end of idle sleep, which does nothing while she is awake), a face `moment` `welcome-back`, and a voice `observe-return`; a desktop wake adds a posture `desktop-reaction` `wake` and a voice `observe-wake`. The conductor performs posture intents only under Natural (`wakeFromSleep` and `reactToDesktop` on the scene), hands the moment to `markMoment` under every driver, and hands the voice intents to its `VoicePort` `observeReturn` under every driver: `observe-wake` as a `wake` observation and `observe-return` as a `return` observation. The situation store folds them in the leader only, and the gate learns of a return only from the mind. An unknown presence reading counts as idle, and no substitute signal stands in for a missing sensor: a `null` desktop snapshot or a `null` idle time never makes the resolver's `away` true, so it never opens an away span and never yields a return. Source: the [host capability contract ADR](../adr/2026-11-08-host-capability-contract.md) owner decisions.
16. One [voice policy](glossary.md#voice-policy) decides every reach-out, every widget reaction, and every spoken touch line, inside `step`, on the mind's clock and its seeded random source, under every driver (`packages/character-mind/src/voice.ts`). `useSituationStore` keeps the 2 s timer in the leader: each tick prunes threads, builds a `VoiceView`, and sends a `voice-tick` to the mind connected through `VoicePort` `connect`. The connected mind is the leader's `useCharacterMindStore` mind (rule 18); with no mind connected, the tick is dropped. The store's `MindVoice` steps the mind and dispatches each `speak` intent to `VoicePort` `speak`, and `publishVoice` keeps the mind's gate memory as the store's read model, carries its user slice (the floor, the hourly cap's fires, and the ritual day) in the run's `VoiceRun.user`, saves the floor and the ritual day, and records the decision for the inspector. Each promotion and each "Delete all data" starts a new run, and each card switch resets the mind: at its first tick of a run the mind restores the gate memory from a promotion with the run's user slice and its own pacing from the slow state laid over it (`restoreGateMemory`), so her pacing follows the card while the floor and the hourly cap stay per user. The urge and its threshold ease in again at every restore. A fire draws five values from the mind's source and a hold draws none, except a `no-topic` hold, which draws five. A user turn reaches the mind as a `user-turn` stimulus. Devtools "Evaluate now" sends a tick with `forceTiming` and answers `no-mind` when no mind is connected.
17. Every unprompted voice goes through the mind; only the user's own turns bypass it. A widget or plugin turn that would make her speak is a `voice-request` stimulus, which `performSparkNotifyReaction` sends through `requestVoice` of `useSituationStore` before the notify enters the situation. Only the leader serves: `App.vue` gives the Spark Notify host role `main` only to the leader window, and user input listeners run only while a window leads. The mind holds the request while anyone is busy (rule 2 and the Widget reaction row), then yields `speak` with a `widget` line; the instruction offers `<|SILENT|>`, so the model keeps its veto. The gate's hard holds and reach-out settings do not apply to it. Its deadlines run on the mind's clock: past `startBy` the mind yields `drop`, and past `speakBy` it yields `cancel`, which `VoicePort` `cancel` turns into a cancelled chat turn. `nextVoiceWakeAt` gives the earliest deadline, and `publishVoice` arms one timer at its `wakeAtMs` that ticks the mind. The caller always gets a `ChatUnpromptedResult`: spoken, declined, cancelled, or failed. A dropped or cancelled request leaves its notify unaddressed in the situation, so she may raise it on her own later as an urgent reach-out. Notices (`notifyConversation`) still speak outside the mind (overlap 12).
18. One mind in the leader. The app constructs one `CharacterMind`, in the unsynced `useCharacterMindStore` (`packages/stage-ui/src/stores/character-mind.ts`), which `App.vue` initializes after the character state and situation stores. The mind outlives stage remounts. On promotion the store waits for `useCharacterStateStore` `ready` and connects to `VoicePort` `connect`; on demotion it disconnects, so the gate runs whether or not a stage is mounted. A mounted `Stage.vue` hands the store's mind to its conductor for touch and desktop steps and lends the store its body through `attachStage`; the store sends the cues of its voice lines to the newest attached stage and drops them with none. A follower window that renders a stage gets its own mind from its own store, which answers that stage's touch and desktop steps but is never connected, so it decides no line. No stimulus or intent crosses windows.

## Target

This section states the design that refactors move toward. It does not describe the code. The [character mind ADR](../adr/2026-11-17-character-mind-direction.md) records the decision. When a refactor lands a rule, the rule moves into the sections above and the overlaps it closes leave the list below.

1. **One mind per character.** Every [decider](glossary.md#decider) in [Deciders](#deciders) becomes part of one [character mind](glossary.md#character-mind) that runs in the leader window, or a realiser of its intents. Mood, idle draws, refractories, and reach-out pacing exist once per character, not once per rendered stage.
2. **One stimulus, one response.** The mind answers each [stimulus](glossary.md#stimulus) once, across every [channel](glossary.md#channel). A beat gets one coordinated response, chosen with every channel's state in view. A touch and a return already get one ([Precedence](#precedence) rules 14 and 15).
3. **One clock.** Natural's idle draw moves onto the mind's clock, which body episodes and the voice channel already share (rule 16).
4. **Episodes in the mind, frames in the performer.** The mind decides discrete episodes. The per-stage [performer](glossary.md#performer-target) runs continuous control. Cursor notice is a mind decision; tracking the cursor is the performer's.
5. **Renderer deciders act on intents.** The MMD body director and any other renderer decider act on the mind's [intents](glossary.md#intent) and on the renderer's own world. They never subscribe to touch, cursor, return, chat, or music directly.
6. **Music is a stimulus.** Under Natural, one detector reports music to the mind, which starts a groove episode, and no decider moves the body on beats outside the mind. Beat sync stays as upstream ships it for Universal and MAGIC.
7. **Only declared capabilities.** The mind issues only the episodes the active performer declares. A refusal comes back as a reported event.
8. **Behavior settings belong to the character.** One character-behavior settings document gates what the mind decides. Renderer pages hold only realisation settings.
9. **Every unprompted voice goes through the mind.** Landed as [Precedence](#precedence) rule 17.

### Rulings the slices keep

Luke made these product rulings in earlier plans. Today they live in frozen ADR bodies and drafts, and the slices rewrite the code that carries them. Each slice keeps every ruling it touches, or records Luke's new ruling here.

- **Cursor** (CM6). She does not always notice the cursor. Sometimes she pays attention to it at medium or far range and sometimes not, as an independent character would. Source: decision D2 of the cursor attention ADR.
- **Voice** (CM3a, CM3b). Code decides when she may speak, and the model decides whether and what: it may decline with `<|SILENT|>`. Silence is the default. Most openings are hers and never follow an app switch. She opens no new topic inside a live exchange. After one unanswered opening only a continuation may open for 90 minutes, and after two only an urgent notification makes her speak for hours. A reply does not restore the rate at once. A follow-up returns once to an open thread. A ritual happens at most once a day, and a missed window stays missed. A decline counts as neutral for backoff. Sources: the [situational presence ADR](../adr/2026-10-19-situational-presence.md) and the [reach-out cadence ADR](../adr/2026-10-29-unprompted-reach-out-cadence.md). The voice policy in `packages/character-mind/src/voice.ts` carries it, composing the gate rules in `stage-shared/situation`.
- **Spoken reactions** (CM3a, CM3b). A spoken reaction is stored in chat history; a spoken-but-forgotten reaction was rejected. A turn may be answered with an expression alone. Source: the nonverbal channels ADR. The voice policy in `packages/character-mind/src/voice.ts` carries it, composing the gate rules in `stage-shared/situation`.
- **Character state** (CM4). The state stays hidden from the user and changes over long timescales, with no daily reset. The relationship cannot be farmed, absence never punishes, and trust is asymmetric. The long-term reward is changed behavior, not a number. The model never sees numbers or two moods, and model cues never feed temperament. Source: the [layered character state ADR](../adr/2026-11-03-layered-character-state.md).

## Known overlaps

Each case below has two or more deciders acting on the same trigger or channel with no single arbiter between them. This list describes the code; it proposes nothing.

- **1. Beat sync and Natural on Live2D head angles.** The `pre` plugin of `useMotionUpdatePluginsBeatSync` writes `ParamAngleX`, `ParamAngleY`, and `ParamAngleZ` in the motion manager's `pre` stage under every driver. Its `layer` plugin, which adds the beat offset in the `final` stage, is inactive under Natural. Under Natural, the adapter frame registered in the `final` stage of `packages/stage-ui-live2d/src/components/scenes/live2d/Model.vue` writes the head afterward. The only ordering is the plugin stage, and neither side knows the other is running.
- **2. Music.** Natural's idle drive carries `music` (from `NaturalIdleDrive`), which weights the sway act in `idleActivityWeights`, while beat sync moves the head on detected beats. Two deciders answer the same audio with separate detectors and no shared state.
- **3. Idle draw and reach-out on separate clocks.** The voice policy runs on the mind's clock and source (rule 16), but each stage's `NaturalIdleActivities` still draws its own hazard on Natural's clock. An idle act can be running when a reach-out fires. The only links are the performance mode (talking ends the act) and the mind's `about-to-speak` cue intent; the gate does not read what she is doing except as reason text (`selfHint`).
- **5. Two rendered stages (latent).** One `useCharacterMindStore` mind serves every stage of a window (rule 18), so two stages in the leader would share one mind, one touch dose path, and one voice connection. Each stage's Natural still draws its own idle activities, until CM4b moves the idle draw into the mind. No build renders two live stages today: one launch provides either `windows:stage` or `windows:main` (`apps/stage-tamagotchi/src/main/index.ts`), only that window is the leader (`resolveRendererWindowContext` maps every other window's `synced-leader=false` query to a follower), and the settings preview (`preview-stage.vue`) has no conductor, touch, or Natural. The overlap becomes live only when a second stage is added.
- **7. Glances.** Emotion impulses, pokes, desktop reactions, idle acts (look-around, peer), and chat cues each call `NaturalGazeLayer` `glance` in `packages/motion-driver-natural/src/gaze.ts`. The character mind decides desktop reactions, but `startPending` still performs them with a direct `glance`. Idle acts check `blocked` before they start, but the others do not check one another; the latest glance replaces the earlier one.
- **8. The cursor on MMD.** Natural's cursor attention turns her gaze to the cursor with no MMD setting to turn it off (`MMDNaturalAdapter` passes the cursor in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/composables/mmd/natural/adapter.ts`), and the body director's chase walks her toward the same cursor within `CHASE_WINDOW_MS` of an interaction (`decideBody`). The gaze rule in `MMDBody` resolves only the gaze channel while she walks; the decision to chase and the decision to look are made separately.
- **9. Sleep on Live2D.** Natural decides sleep (`NaturalIdleActivities`), `useIdleProps` shows the sleep expression entries 5 s after she starts to fall asleep, and the conductor always sends `allowSleep: true` (`StagePerformanceConductor`). The two follow one report stream; nothing stops a model's own idle motion group under [Universal](glossary.md#universal) from showing a different state, because Universal gets no drive at all.
- **11. Two touch systems, split by driver.** The fork's touch reaction (the Touch reaction row above, one response that the mind decides under Natural) and upstream's touch reaction, touch motion, and avatar touch notify answer the same pointer gestures. Only the motion driver chooses between them, and four places read it: `Live2D.vue` (`touchGesturesActive`), `Model.vue` (`touchActive`), `Stage.vue` (`onAvatarTouch`), and `StagePerformanceConductor` (`touch`). The two systems recognize gestures with separate trackers (`NaturalTouchGestures` and `createLive2DTouchGestureTracker`) and pace speech with separate cooldowns: `REACTION_REFRACTORY_MS` (45 s) after a spoken line in the mind under Natural, 20 s for each region under the other drivers. The `touch` setting of the Live2D page gates only the Natural side.
- **12. Notice turns speak outside the gate.** `notifyConversation` starts a chat turn whose reply speaks, without the urge gate, its hard suppressors, its floor, or its hourly cap, and without the presence settings that turn reach-outs off. It is not an unprompted turn, so it can run while one is pending. The gate's floor does not start from it through `recordExternalSpeech`. The model may stay quiet, and the owner started the task that led to it.
- **13. Two notify sources about touch and time reach the gate unranked.** An avatar touch notify and a task reminder both enter the situation as notifies with urgency `immediate`, beside plugin notifies. The gate ranks an urgent notify above the urge path, so a touch under Universal or MAGIC takes the same path as an urgent plugin event. They reach the mind's voice policy as urgent notifies in the `VoiceView`.
