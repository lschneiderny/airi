# Autonomous behavior

This file describes the code on `main`. A change that makes a statement here false updates this file in the same commit. These rules win over the ADRs in `docs/ai/adr/`; the ADRs record the history behind them. When an ADR and this file disagree, this file describes the code.

This file lists every piece of code that makes the character do something nobody asked for, the order that applies today when two of them want the same moment or body part, and the places where two of them act on the same trigger or channel without one arbiter. Code is cited by repository path and symbol, never by line number. Terms follow the [domain glossary](glossary.md).

## Deciders

Times are seconds of [Natural](glossary.md#natural)'s active-motion clock unless they carry a `_MS` name or say ms. "Live2D page" is `packages/stage-ui/src/components/scenarios/settings/model-settings/live2d.vue`, "MMD page" is `packages/stage-ui/src/components/scenarios/settings/model-settings/mmd.vue`, and "Presence page" is `packages/stage-pages/src/pages/settings/presence/index.vue`. Every Natural [decider](glossary.md#decider) runs only while the [motion driver](glossary.md#motion-driver) is Natural.

## Precedence

The order applied today, from the outermost arbiter inward. Each step names the code that applies it.

1. A user turn always runs. It bypasses the gate, and the gate holds while the user speaks or types, while a turn generates, and while sends wait in the queue (`hardReason` in `packages/stage-shared/src/situation/gate.ts`).
2. One unprompted turn at a time. A reach-out and a spoken touch reaction share the chat store's single pending unprompted turn (`useChatStore` `submitUnprompted` and `submitReaction` in `packages/stage-ui/src/stores/chat.ts`). The gate skips evaluation while one is pending, and a reaction that finds one pending is rolled back without spending its retry wait (`useSituationStore` in `packages/stage-ui/src/stores/situation/index.ts`).
3. Within the urge gate: hard suppressors, then an urgent notify, then a due double text, then the urge path with holds in the order `floor`, `quiet-unanswered`, `safety-cap`, `awaiting-reply`, `urge`, `no-topic` (`evaluateUrgeGate`). A spoken touch reaction starts the gate's floor (`recordExternalSpeech`).
4. Natural performs only under the Natural driver. The conductor sends no idle drive to any other driver (`StagePerformanceConductor` `idleDrive`), and the MMD scene sends a rate of 0 while a clip action plays (`resolveIdleDrive` in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/components/scenes/MMD.vue`).
5. Inside one Natural grid step (`NaturalMotion` in `packages/motion-driver-natural/src/natural-motion.ts`):
   - The gaze goes to an active pet override first, then the long-pet camera glance, then a chat glance, then cursor attention (its weight scaled down by the pet level), then free fixation.
   - Cursor attention is dismissed while its setting is off, attention is `aside`, or attention is `screen`.
   - Pending actions start in order: cue gestures, mood doses, pokes, emotion episodes, desktop reactions (`startPending`). An emotion gesture does not start while a cue gesture holds the head.
   - Idle activities come last. They are blocked by the layers above and interrupted by any new poke, emotion, cue gesture, or noticed cursor.
6. Within the idle draw: a follow-up stretch or yawn after sleep goes before a new draw; acts and errands then share one hazard by rate; an `idle-clip` waits for a running act, while a pastime and a wander run beside acts (`NaturalIdleActivities`).
7. On Live2D expressions, slot leases from the model's requests outrank idle claims (pastimes, sleep entries, moments), which outrank the user's manual switches; a manual change drops the claims (`useIdleProps` in `packages/stage-ui/src/composables/performance/use-idle-props.ts`).
8. On the MMD body (`decideBody` in `packages/motion-driver-humanoid/src/director/index.ts`):
   - A request from any source other than `idle` replaces an unasked clip. An unasked clip is refused while anything performs, while she walks, and while she talks, listens, or thinks. Any clip is refused while she is away or asleep, or while the user drags her.
   - A performing clip other than a gesture forces a stand over sit or sleep; otherwise Natural's `asleep` maps to the sleep stance or a doze, and `settled` maps to sit or a rest, only at home and at rest.
   - Goal requests: debug over tool over chat unfold. Then a held goal, then the cursor chase, then return home, then entering a zone from a wing, then a wander or visit. While the user is active (`watching`), a wander only visits.
9. On the MMD gaze, walking outranks everything: travel direction, then the cursor while Natural fixates it, then the chat, then free fixation around the camera (`MMDBody` in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/composables/mmd/body/index.ts`).

## Known overlaps

Each case below has two or more deciders acting on the same trigger or channel with no single arbiter between them. This list describes the code; it proposes nothing.

1. **Beat sync and Natural on Live2D head angles.** `useMotionUpdatePluginBeatSync` writes `ParamAngleX`, `ParamAngleY`, and `ParamAngleZ` in the motion manager's `pre` stage under every driver. Under Natural, the adapter frame registered in the `final` stage of `packages/stage-ui-live2d/src/components/scenes/live2d/Model.vue` writes the head afterward. The only ordering is the plugin stage, and neither side knows the other is running.
2. **Music.** Natural's idle drive carries `music` (from `NaturalIdleDrive`), which weights the sway act in `idleActivityWeights`, while beat sync moves the head on detected beats. Two deciders answer the same audio with separate detectors and no shared state.
3. **Idle draw and reach-out on separate clocks.** Each stage's `NaturalIdleActivities` draws its own hazard, and the leader's `evaluateUrgeGate` draws another. An idle act can be running when a reach-out fires. The only links are the performance mode (talking ends the act) and the `about-to-speak` cue sent from `Stage.vue`; the gate does not read what she is doing except as reason text (`selfHint`).
4. **One touch, five reactions.** A poke or pet reaches Natural's pet and poke response in the scene (`startPending`, `NaturalPetLayer`), a mood dose through `useStageTouchStore` and `touchAffect`, a moment expression on Live2D (`useIdleProps` `onMoment` from `Stage.vue`), the spoken reaction policy on the leader (`reactionPolicy`), and on MMD a body reaction (`BodyReactionKind` in `packages/motion-driver-humanoid/src/performance/contracts.ts`). Each has its own cooldown (2 s poke dedupe, 1 s and 4 s dose cooldowns, 60 s entry rest, 45 s spoken refractory), and no arbiter weighs them together.
5. **Two rendered stages.** `useStageTouchStore` documents that each rendered stage owns its own conductor, so touching two stages doses mood twice and sends the leader one observation per stage. Each stage's Natural also draws its own idle activities.
6. **The user comes back.** One return can start the `welcome-back` moment expression (the `performance.value.away` watcher in `Stage.vue`), a desktop `wake` reaction when the desktop was locked or asleep (`resolvePerformanceState`), Natural's wake from idle sleep (`NaturalIdleActivities`), and a `welcome-back` ritual reach-out (`RITUAL_TUNING` in `packages/stage-shared/src/situation/reasons/types.ts`). Each decides on its own.
7. **Glances.** Emotion impulses, pokes, desktop reactions, idle acts (look-around, peer), and chat cues each call `NaturalGazeLayer` `glance` in `packages/motion-driver-natural/src/gaze.ts`. Idle acts check `blocked` before they start, but the others do not check one another; the latest glance replaces the earlier one.
8. **The cursor on MMD.** Natural's cursor attention turns her gaze to the cursor with no MMD setting to turn it off (`MMDNaturalAdapter` passes the cursor in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/composables/mmd/natural/adapter.ts`), and the body director's chase walks her toward the same cursor within `CHASE_WINDOW_MS` of an interaction (`decideBody`). The gaze rule in `MMDBody` resolves only the gaze channel while she walks; the decision to chase and the decision to look are made separately.
9. **Sleep on Live2D.** Natural decides sleep (`NaturalIdleActivities`), `useIdleProps` shows the sleep expression entries 5 s after she starts to fall asleep, and the conductor always sends `allowSleep: true` (`StagePerformanceConductor`). The two follow one report stream; nothing stops a model's own idle motion group under [Universal](glossary.md#universal) from showing a different state, because Universal gets no drive at all.

## Non-deciders in the search

`git grep -lE 'Exp\(1\)|hazard|cooldown' -- 'packages/*/src/*' ':!*.test.ts'` also lists files that decide nothing about her behavior:

- `packages/stage-shared/src/perf/usage/incidents.ts`: cooldowns of performance incident reports, not character behavior.
- `packages/qa-tools/src/stage/measure.ts` and the comment in `packages/stage-ui-mmd/src/components/scenes/MMD.vue` about the gesture repeat cooldown: QA tooling that replays gestures.
- `packages/stage-pages/src/pages/devtools/situation/components/situation-gate.vue` and `packages/stage-ui/src/stores/devtools/situation-observability.ts`: devtools views of the urge gate's hazards.
- `packages/stage-shared/src/character-state/urge.ts` and `packages/stage-shared/src/character-state/types.ts`: the mood factor that the reach-out row already lists.
- `packages/i18n/src/locales/en/tamagotchi/settings.yaml`: labels.
