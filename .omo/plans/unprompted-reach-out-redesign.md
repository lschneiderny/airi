## TL;DR (For humans)

**Who this is for and what changes for them:** You, at your desk with her in the corner of the screen. Today she pipes up about every ten minutes, always about whatever app you just switched to. After this, she messages you a few times a day the way a girlfriend texts: to follow up on something you said, to say good morning or good night, to welcome you back, or just about her own day, with hours of silence in between and no fixed rhythm.

**What you'll get:** A rewritten decision engine that spaces her messages randomly around an average you control with the existing Chattiness slider; new reasons for her to reach out (open threads pulled from your last conversation, daily rituals, her own day); a prompt that asks her whether she would actually text you right now, with staying quiet as the normal answer; a reworked inspector page so the behavior can be tuned from real days; updated docs and tests; delivered as a reviewed pull request.

**Why this approach:** A cooldown can only make a metronome slower, so timing becomes a random process with a target average instead of a minimum gap. Her reasons stop being your screen: screen events can no longer start a message except a long session, your return, or a reminder, and everything she opens with comes from threads, rituals, or herself. State survives a reload so she never greets you twice.

**What it will NOT do:** No new settings switches. Nothing about the background thread extraction appears in your chat. She still goes quiet exactly as today for Do Not Disturb, calls, games, typing, and a hidden window, and plugins keep working. No memory across days yet (the store is shaped for it), and no scripted daily life for her; she improvises her day when she speaks.
