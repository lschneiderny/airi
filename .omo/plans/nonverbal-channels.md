**Who this is for and what changes for them:** You, with her in the corner of your screen. Today petting or poking her moves the picture for two seconds and nothing else, every answer is a paragraph of speech, and she cannot answer without words. After this, a pet or a poke changes her mood and her next words know it; now and then she says one short line about it (a soft word after a long pet, a playful protest at the third poke); she can answer a message with just a look or a nod; and in texting mode she sends two or three short messages in a row with text faces like :3, the way a Discord regular would.

**What you'll get:** Touch wired into her mind (mood, awareness, and an occasional one-line reaction that never talks over you and never turns into a reach-out); wordless replies that still leave a small mark in the chat so your message never looks ignored; five gestures she can use (nod, shake, shrug, lean, glance); a Conversation setting for Talking or Texting with a Plain or Discord flavor; message bursts with pauses in texting mode; text faces that show in bubbles but are never spoken; inspector panels and lab buttons to tune it; scoring in the model-eval tooling; docs; delivered as a reviewed pull request.

**Why this approach:** Her reach-out system is tuned in minutes and would make a poke protest arrive late or never, so touch gets its own small, deterministic policy and the reach-out system only learns to wait after she speaks. Everything else reuses what already exists: the reaction is an ordinary unprompted turn, gestures build on the clip layer the emotions already use, a burst is one message shown as several bubbles, and the register changes one section of her prompt without changing the versioned contract that the model tests key on.

**What it will NOT do:** No touch topic in the reach-out system, no new plugin-facing turn type, no character-card format change, no changes to the Discord bot. No Unicode emoji anywhere; text faces are shown, never spoken. Playback pauses for DELAY are not fixed here (they never paused; recorded as a follow-up).
