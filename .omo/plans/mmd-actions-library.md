## TL;DR (For humans)
<!-- Fill this LAST, after the detailed plan below is written, so it summarizes the REAL plan. -->
<!-- Plain English for a non-engineer: NO file paths, NO todo numbers, NO wave/agent/tool names. -->

**Who this is for and what changes for them:** You, with an MMD character on your Windows desktop. Today a motion file you import is just a name you can pin to an emotion or a stance. After this, every imported clip is understood on import (is it a short upper-body gesture, a full-body action, a dance, or an idle pose?), you give it a short label and decide whether the AI may use it, and the character can wave mid-sentence while she keeps walking, or stop, stand up from her chair and dance when asked, then go back to whatever she was doing.

**What you'll get:** A clip library with derived kinds, labels and an "AI may use" switch in the model settings; two ways for the AI to use it (a short gesture written into its speech cues, and a `perform_action` request for full-body actions that she may politely refuse while busy, asleep or being dragged); gestures that layer over idle, sitting and walking instead of replacing them; a Motion Lab page that lists the clips and lets you play or stop any of them; two new stage quality gates and model-eval scoring for the new channel; the roadmap and a design record inside the repository; and a measured answer to whether reading other windows' rectangles is cheap enough to build the next stage on.

**Why this approach:** The body director stays the only thing that decides what she does; a clip is one more request it accepts or refuses, so walking, sitting, chasing and clips never fight. Clips are split per body part when they load, because the animation mixer cannot mask bones and the current play path would otherwise replace her idle or stand her up. Dances play once because the physics engine resets on every loop. Labels go into the live part of the prompt, so the pinned contract text and its hash do not move.

**What it will NOT do:** No beat detection or tempo-matched dancing. No window sensor beyond the measurement, no perching, pointing, props or dangling. No motion files added to the repository, no new dependencies, nothing merged into the main branch.

**Effort:** Large

**Risk:** Medium - the layered play path (splitting clips per body part and partitioning the base) changes how every clip blends, and the ducking fix touches foot contact while walking; both are gated by the existing skate and feet checks on the real stage.
**Decisions to sanity-check:** Exposure is off by default for every clip until you switch it on. Action and dance refractories are 15 s and 60 s counted from the end of the clip; a gesture repeats after 4 s. A full-body action asked for while she is speaking waits until she stops; a gesture plays at once. A gesture never ends a walk; a full-body clip pauses it and resumes the goal afterwards; any poke, drag, chat opening or move request ends a looping clip. Neutral and sleepy emotions no longer replay the idle clip.
