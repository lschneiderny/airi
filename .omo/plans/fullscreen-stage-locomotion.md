**Who this is for and what changes for them:** You, with an MMD character on your Windows desktop. Instead of living inside a small box, she stands on the taskbar line of your screen with a home spot, walks over when you open the chat, follows the cursor for a while after you poke or pet her, wanders now and then if you let her, walks back home, and her arms and hands hang naturally instead of the stiff pose the model ships with. Your Live2D character and the old window layout keep working exactly as before.

**What you'll get:** A see-through full-screen stage that only takes clicks where she is drawn; a real 3D floor and camera so her feet always sit on the taskbar line at the size you choose; walking that never slides her feet; relaxed arms, hands and fingers that give way to any imported motion that moves them; chat that docks beside her home; three small things the AI can ask for (come here, go home, look at); settings for stage mode, size, home display and roaming; a developer lab page showing all of it; and a measured performance check on your heaviest model before any of it is built on.

**Why this approach:** Measure the stage first, because a full-screen transparent window is the one cost nobody has numbers for. Keep every decision (where to go, when to sit, whether to roam) in one pure, testable director, and keep the walking math and the arm pose in the same shared package that already drives her feet and gaze, so the same code can serve a VRM body later. Move her whole model through the scene before the hair and cloth simulation runs, so travel feels physical, and never reset the simulation per frame.

**What it will NOT do:** No walking between monitors, no sitting on windows, no props, no dance or gesture library, no VRM, no Live2D on the stage, no relationship memory, nothing in front of or behind the screen plane yet. No model or motion files are added to the repository. Nothing merges to the main branch from this plan.
